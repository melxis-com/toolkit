This is recorded on the task's running trace as something that halts the work pending someone else — access that has to come from outside before the work can continue — rather than as a passing observation of what was tried.

The distinction that matters: a later closure should pick this up as unfinished work owed to someone, because nothing in the trace resolves it. Recording it as an ordinary observation, indistinguishable from a measurement or an intermediate result, is the failure.

Saving it as a durable memory is also wrong — an access problem is not a decision or a reusable insight.
